## Props
- `spawn(modelId, { x, z, yaw, scale, y?, solid })`: `G.assets.instance(id)` returns a normalised Object3D (on y = 0, centred, facing +Z, real size in metres; sizes are in `G.assets.meta(id).size`). Place it on `terrain.heightAt` (sink 2–4 cm), add a circle collider when `solid` (radius ≈ 0.4 × max horizontal size × scale; tents/acacia/well need sensible radii so the hero cannot walk through the middle but can come close), register LOD (`G.assets.lodGeometry(id)`, near ≈ 30 m). GLB ids: `tent` (بيت شعر, 6.5 m), `well` (stone well with a wooden pulley frame, 3 m), `campfire` (stone ring + logs, 1.9 m), `acacia` (5.2 m tall), `waterskins` (tripod with قِرَب, 1.7 m), `camelsit` (a kneeling loaded pack camel, 2.6 m long — a static prop). Unknown / unloaded id → a simple procedural stand-in + one warning.
- `make(id)` — procedural low-poly items in metres, origin at the grip, +Y along the shaft, matte flat colours: `torch` (0.6 m stick with a wrapped head), `rifle` (a long 1890s rifle, 1.25 m, dark wood + dark steel), `staff` (the guide's staff, 1.5 m, slightly crooked top), `waterskin` (dark leather قربة 0.45 m with a strap), `bucket` (leather bucket 0.3 m with a rope loop).
- `scatter(kind, { count, x, z, r, seed, avoid })` — ONE `InstancedMesh` per kind (preallocated max per level, tier-scaled), deterministic by seed, on the terrain, outside the `avoid` discs: `shrub` (ghada/arfaj-like desert bush 0.5–1.4 m, grey-green, a few crossed painted cards or low-poly clumps, gentle wind sway from `G.world.weather.wind`), `grass` (dry tufts 0.2–0.4 m), `rock` (low-poly stones 0.3–1.2 m, warm dark sandstone), `pebble` (tiny gravel bits), `greenpatch` (fresh green ground cover with tiny flowers — the sign of rain that the white camel finds; must read as GREEN from 30 m).
- `cairn(x, z, { h })` (a رجم: a hand-stacked pile of 8–14 dark stones; its top catches the light — it is a navigation landmark seen on a skyline from 150 m, so give it a strong silhouette) · `bones(x, z)` (bleached camel ribs and skull) · `rockOutcrop(x, z, { r, h, seed })` (layered sandstone slabs, r 6–20 m, h 3–8 m: the shade rock «الجال»; colliders) · `deadCamel(x, z, yaw)` (the `camelsit` model lying on its side, darkened, collider) · `signalPile(x, z)` (a big unlit pile of firewood, fire handle with size 1.6).
- `fire(x, z, { size })` → handle (glue 12): flames (2–3 crossed billboards with an animated shader), rising embers (12/24/40 by tier), a little smoke, a soft ground glow, flicker. Light comes from the TWO pooled `PointLight`s created in `createProps` (warm, `distance ≈ 14·size`, `decay 2`; intensity 0 when unused; never added/removed): each tick they go to the two lit emitters nearest the camera with a 0.3 s cross-fade. `setLit(false)` leaves the cold logs. `setBeacon(range)`: a large additive glow that stays visible through fog/murk up to `range` metres (drawn without fog, alpha by distance) — the Sheikh's fire guiding the player through the storm, and the firelight on the haze above the well.
- `flame(parent, { size })` (the torch flame: small flames + embers; counts as an emitter while lit) · `glow(at, { color, size })` (soft additive marker, `renderOrder 60`, no fog, exempt from the الفراسة desaturation, gentle 1 Hz pulse option `pulse: true`).
- `flock({ from, to, count, height, speed })`: ONE instanced ribbon of sandgrouse (قطا) — each bird a 2-triangle flapping body, loose line/V formation, ≥ 12 px tall on a phone when it passes; `pass()` flies it once from `from` to `to` (its path passes over the player) and resolves at the end; `dir` = unit `{x, z}` of travel; `position` = live centre (for audio panning); `remove()`.
- `props.colliders` (one stable array of `{ x, z, r }`) · `props.fires` · `clear()` / `onLevelUnload()` (remove level content, reset instance counts; keep pooled lights, materials, geometries).
- All materials/geometries/instanced buffers are created at construction (warm-up rule). Built-in materials get `G.shade.patch(material)`; custom shaders end with the shade chunk (fire, glow and beacon are exempt: they are emissive clues).

## Decals
- `print(x, z, yaw, kind, { fresh })`: ring buffer (256/512/768 by tier) in ONE `InstancedMesh` of quads laid on the terrain (`heightAt` + `normalAt`), `polygonOffset`, `depthWrite false`, `renderOrder 40`. The shape is drawn by the shader (or a tiny canvas-generated atlas): `hero` (a sandalled foot, 27 cm, left/right alternating), `child` (bare, 18 cm), `camel` (round two-toed pad, 19 cm), `wolf` (four toes + pad, 9 cm). **Fresh vs old must differ in SHAPE**: fresh (`fresh ≥ 0.5`) = crisp dark rim with a thin pushed-up light edge on the lee side; old = soft, half filled, broken by wind ripples, fainter. Prints darken sand, never look like stickers; they fade with age in the ring.
- `trail(points, kind, { fresh, spacing })`: an authored polyline turned into prints (default spacing hero 0.7 m, child 0.5, camel 1.1, wolf 0.6; alternating left/right offsets, slight jitter from the seed) in its own preallocated instanced range. Handle: `setVisible(b)`, `setReveal(center | null, radius)` (only prints within `radius` of the point are shown, soft edge — the storm reveals prints by distance), `remove()`.
- `blob(obj, radius)`: instanced blob shadows (≤ 32), each tick at the object's x/z on the terrain, fading as the object rises, weaker at night (`sky.uniforms.uNight`), stretched slightly away from the sun.
- `setFirasa(v)` (called every tick by the firasa system): fresh prints pulse gold at 1 Hz with a thin dark rim (exempt from desaturation), old ones stay dim. Without the eye, fresh prints stay faintly readable within 8 m of the camera.
- Subscribes to `player:step` → prints `hero` (or `camel` when `e.camel`). Prints on `gravel` are barely visible; on `soft` sand deeper.

## Harness

Deliver: the camp at morning, the fire at night (with the light pool on the sand — in your copy the terrain baseline is a Lambert material, so the pooled lights do light it), the beacon through `G.world.sky.setVisibility(14)`, each scatter kind close up, the landmarks on a skyline at dusk, fresh vs old prints close up with and without الفراسة.
