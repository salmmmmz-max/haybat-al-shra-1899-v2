# Module part — SKY, STARS & WEATHER

**Owned files**: `game/src/world/sky.js` · `game/src/world/weather.js` · `game/src/world/stars-data.js` (catalogue) · `game/harness/sky.js` · `game/harness/weather.js` · `game/tests/unit/sky.test.js`.
Contract: ARCHITECTURE §4 `sky.js`, `weather.js`; rendering rules §1; glue items 5, 11. The current files are throw-away baselines.

## Sky
- ONE dome mesh (inverted sphere or a far-plane full-screen triangle), `renderOrder 20`, `depthWrite false`, `frustumCulled false`, follows the camera. Gradient zenith → horizon, a warm horizon glow around the sun azimuth, sun disc + soft halo (big and soft when low). Subtle painterly cloud wisps are welcome if cheap (one small shared noise texture; no `sin`-hash on large coordinates).
- Eight presets: `dawn`, `morning`, `noon`, `storm`, `dusk`, `night`, `predawn`, `sunrise_rain`. You design the colour tables. `storm` = yellow-brown murk with a darker wall rising on the windward horizon (read `G.world.weather.wind` lazily at update time; the wall stands where the wind comes FROM). `sunrise_rain` = a low grey-violet cloud deck over the west, a bright clear east. `night` = moonless, starlit, stylised: deep blue-black zenith, slightly lighter horizon — people and dunes must remain readable as cool silhouettes on a phone screen outdoors (do not make the night too dark).
- Lighting (the whole game's look depends on it): `sky.sunLight` (DirectionalLight, no shadows; follows the camera) and `sky.hemi`. With `NoToneMapping`, a white surface facing the key light must stay ≤ ~1.0: tune `sunLight.intensity·colour + hemi` per preset. Day: strong warm key, cool ambient (clear light side / shadow side on dunes and faces). Night: a dim cool key from high up so forms keep their modelling (total ≈ 0.3), warm firelight comes from the pooled point lights of `props`. At night `sunDir` is this key-light direction and the sun disc is hidden; `sunBearing()` returns `null`.
- Shared uniforms object `sky.uniforms` (exact names in the contract; colours linear, `uSunColor` premultiplied by intensity). Other shaders hold these BY REFERENCE: never replace the `{ value }` objects, only mutate them.
- Fog: you create the one `THREE.FogExp2` on `G.scene` and are its only writer. `setVisibility(m | null)`: density = max(preset density, 1.98 / m); the colour blends toward the preset's murk colour as visibility shrinks. `scene.background` = horizon colour (fallback behind the dome).
- `setPreset(name, { duration })` / `stars.setLST(h, { duration })`: transitions advance in `sky.update(dt)` (sim time); the returned promise is `G.scope.until(() => finished)` (abort-safe; with `duration 0` apply immediately and return a resolved promise). A new call supersedes a running one. Sun moves on a great circle (slerp), colours lerp in linear space.

## Stars (real sky, observer at 20.0° N)
- `stars-data.js`: ≥ 60 named bright stars — id, RA (h), Dec (°), magnitude, colour (B−V or RGB) — including all of: `polaris dubhe merak phecda megrez alioth mizar alkaid canopus sirius` plus the usual first-magnitude stars (vega, arcturus, capella, rigel, betelgeuse, procyon, altair, aldebaran, antares, spica, pollux, castor, fomalhaut, deneb, regulus, achernar, bellatrix, alnilam, …). Plus ~1500 faint procedural stars (deterministic; denser along a soft Milky Way band).
- Horizontal coordinates from RA/Dec + LST + latitude: hour angle `H = LST − RA`; then alt/az; world direction for azimuth `az` (from north, clockwise) and altitude `alt`: `(sin az · cos alt, sin alt, −cos az · cos alt)` (north = −Z, east = +X). Put this maths in a pure exported function and unit-test it.
- Expected values (unit tests, tolerance in brackets): at `LST 18.0` → polaris alt 19.3–20.8°, az within 1.5° of north; dubhe alt 11.1° (±0.6), az 332° (±1.5). At `LST 3.3` → canopus alt 6.9° (±0.4), az 153.7° (±0.8); sirius alt 27.3° (±0.6), az 122° (±1).
- Rendering: `THREE.Points` on the dome (size by magnitude and device pixel ratio, round soft sprites, per-star colour: Suhail/canopus yellowish, sirius blue-white), gentle twinkle (stronger near the horizon), horizon extinction, hidden by storm murk, alpha by `uNight`. `renderOrder 30`.
- `stars.setLST` always advances forward (add 24 h if the target is behind) — the chapter-3 time-lapse wheels the whole sky about the pole in ~12 s.
- `stars.dir(id, out)` (current world unit vector) · `stars.asterism('banat_nash')` = `[[alkaid,mizar],[mizar,alioth],[alioth,megrez],[megrez,phecda],[phecda,merak],[merak,dubhe],[dubhe,megrez]]` · `stars.asterism('pointer')` = `[[merak,dubhe],[dubhe,polaris]]` · `stars.highlight(id | null, amount)`: `id` is a star id (a larger soft blue-white glow, amount 0..1) or an asterism id (its lines draw in progressively with amount 0..1, thin blue-white lines); several highlights may be active at once; `highlight(id, 0)` removes one, `highlight(null)` clears all. All highlight geometry is preallocated.

## Weather
- `setWind(deg, strength)` per glue 11; `weather.wind` = one reused `{ x, z, strength }`.
- `setDust(a, {duration})`: thin airborne haze + ground-hugging sand streamers that snake along the dune surface with the wind (use `terrain.heightAt`; a handful of instanced ribbons) — the signature motion of the desert floor, visible in every daytime chapter at low strength.
- `storm(level, {duration})`: `visibility` falls to ≈ 12 m at level 1 (pushed to `sky.setVisibility` every tick; `Infinity` → `null`); fast grains streaking across the view (points), ≤ 2 full-screen translucent sheets, streamers everywhere. The wall itself is fog colour, not overdraw. Must look violent yet keep ≥ 30 fps on a phone: obey the particle budgets of §11.
- `rain(level, {duration})`: streaks + small ground splashes around the camera (the game's last image is the first rain on the sand — make it gentle and beautiful at level 0.3–0.6).
- `gust({ warn, strength, duration })` → Promise through `G.scope`: emits `weather:gustWarn { in: warn, dir: {x, z} }` at once and builds the visual warning from the windward side during `warn` seconds; at impact emits `weather:gust { strength, duration }`, surges the particles, sets `weather.gusting`; resolves when the gust ends. `onBeatEnd()` cancels a pending gust and settles. No free-running gust timer.
- Everything is created at construction with counts/alpha 0 (warm-up rule), camera-relative, tier-scaled (`setQuality`), `renderOrder 50`, `depthWrite false`, `frustumCulled false`, uses three's fog chunks where appropriate, shade chunk last.
